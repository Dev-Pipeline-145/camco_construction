About Camco Construction

The Camco Story - Built on Integrity Since 1976.

Camco Construction was founded in Spokane on a simple standard: do what you say, build it right, and stand behind the work. Nearly five decades later, that standard still drives every project we take on.

Our History

Our History.

Camco Construction was founded in Spokane in 1976 by Lee Campbell and Anna Campbell. What began as a local general contracting business grew through repeat relationships, referrals, and a reputation for dependable execution in both residential and commercial work.

Over the years, Camco expanded into complex remodels, tenant improvements, excavation support, and accessibility-focused construction for homeowners, businesses, and veterans. Today, Leif Challender (President) and Amy Challender (Vice President) continue that family-led model with hands-on project oversight and direct client communication.
