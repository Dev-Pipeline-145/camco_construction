**Composite Decking (Trex, TimberTech, etc.)**  
Higher upfront cost, minimal maintenance, fade and stain resistance, and 25–30 year warranties. Best long-term value for most Spokane homeowners. Camco installs all major composite brands.

### [H2]
ADA Ramp Standards: What Spokane Homeowners & Businesses Need to Know

Understanding the basic requirements helps you have a more informed conversation with your contractor. Here are the key ADA standards that govern ramp design:

**Slope:** Maximum 1:12 ratio — for every inch of vertical rise, the ramp must be at least 12 inches long. Gentler slopes (1:16 or 1:20) are preferred where space allows.

**Width:** Minimum 36 inches clear width between handrails for commercial applications. Residential ramps may be narrower depending on jurisdiction.

**Landings:** Required at the top and bottom of every ramp run, and at any change in direction. Minimum 60 x 60 inches for commercial; residential dimensions vary.

**Handrails:** Required on both sides of ramps with a rise greater than 6 inches for commercial applications. Residential requirements vary by local code.

**Surface:** Must be stable, firm, and slip-resistant. Open-grid surfaces are not permitted.

Camco designs every ramp to meet or exceed these standards and manages all permit and inspection requirements.
