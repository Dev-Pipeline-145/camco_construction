FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions - Wheelchair Ramp Installation Spokane WA.

How much does a wheelchair ramp cost in Spokane WA?

Residential ramps often range from about $2,500-$12,000+ depending on rise, layout, materials, and structure.

Do I need a permit for a wheelchair ramp in Spokane?

Permit requirements depend on permanence and jurisdiction; Camco confirms and manages the full process.

What is the correct wheelchair ramp slope?

Maximum ADA slope is typically 1:12. Camco designs to meet or exceed required standards.

Do you work with veteran organizations?

Yes. Camco has completed veteran accessibility projects and supports VA-related and community referrals.
